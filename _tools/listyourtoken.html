What happens next

  1. Your payment is verified on-chain before the request is accepted. Nothing is stored until it clears.
  2. A person reviews the issuer link, the metadata, the liquidity and whether the project is findable outside our site.
  3. We reply either way. If something needs changing, we say what.
  4. Approved, the listing goes live. Declined, the fee is returned in full and you are welcome to reapply once the cause is fixed.

Before applying it is worth having the domain handshake resolving and a stellar.toml published — they are free, and they are most of what review looks at.
