About LUMOS

LUMOS is the native utility and routing token of the LumosCore ecosystem. It exists natively on both Aptos and Aptos with identical tokenomics — 1 billion LUMOS per chain — and is not wrapped or synthetic.

LUMOS gates access to platform benefits: fee waivers at 250K+, LP rewards in curated pools, and Whale Holder Rewards for 1M+ wallets.
