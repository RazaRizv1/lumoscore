Cross-chain transfers move value from Stellar to 52 other networks. LumosCore does not run a bridge and never holds your funds in transit — every transfer is carried by one of four routes, and you see which one, what arrives and what it costs before you sign.

The four routes

RouteCarriesReachesArrival
Circle CCTP USDC 17 networks, including Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and Avalanche. You claim it on the destination.
LayerZero USDT0 21 networks — 14 that CCTP cannot reach, including Hyperliquid, Monad, Berachain and Hedera. Delivered for you, in about 30 minutes.
NEAR Intents XLM or USDC in, the token you choose out — BTC, SOL, native XRP and more. 21 further networks, including Bitcoin, Solana, Tron, TON and the XRP Ledger. Delivered for you, in about 30 seconds.
Axelar SHX, as itself A second way to Ethereum, Solana and the XRP Ledger. Delivered for you, in about 5 minutes.

Each route carries one thing, so whatever you send is swapped into it first, at a rate shown before you sign. Axelar is the exception: SHX crosses as itself.

Making a transfer

  1. Open Cross-chain, choose the destination network, then the asset and the amount.
  2. Review the route, what will arrive, the fees and the timing. Where more than one route reaches a network, you choose which to use.
  3. Approve in your wallet. How many signatures depends on the route: a swap into the route's asset is its own signature, and so is the platform fee.
  4. Wait for it to arrive — or, on CCTP, claim it on the destination.

Only CCTP needs a claim

CCTP burns USDC on Stellar, and the USDC is minted on the destination by a transaction you send there, paying that chain's gas. On Ethereum mainnet at a busy moment that can cost more than the transfer fee — consider a cheaper destination such as Base or Arbitrum if you have the choice. LayerZero, NEAR Intents and Axelar deliver for you, with no claim and no gas needed on arrival.

Sending SHX through Axelar? On the XRP Ledger the receiving address needs an SHX trustline first, which holds 0.2 XRP of reserve, and on Solana an SHX token account. Ethereum needs nothing.

If you close the tab

Nothing is stranded. For a CCTP transfer, the burn and Circle's attestation are saved as soon as they exist and listed under Awaiting redemption on Cross-chain. Reconnect the same wallet and finish the claim. An unclaimed transfer does not expire.

LayerZero, NEAR Intents and Axelar arrive on their own, whether or not the tab is open. If a NEAR Intents solver cannot complete a transfer, your deposit is returned.

What it costs

  • LumosCore: 0.2% of the amount transferred, or 0.1% on the LUMOS tier.
  • The route: Circle charges nothing for CCTP. LayerZero and Axelar charge a delivery fee in XLM, quoted before you sign. NEAR Intents quotes one all-in rate from its solvers before you deposit.
  • The networks: Stellar's network fee on each signature, and on CCTP the destination chain's gas to claim.

What can still go wrong

Delegating the transport does not remove transport risk. While a transfer is in flight, it depends on the security and operation of the route carrying it. LumosCore's design bounds the exposure — we hold no pooled balance, so a failure is limited to transfers actually in flight — but it does not eliminate it. See Security.

Check the destination address

Sending to an address you do not control, or to a contract that cannot receive the asset, is not reversible by anyone. Confirm the destination before you sign.
