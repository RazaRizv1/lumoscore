The LUMOS tier

Hold 250,000 LUMOS or more and the swap and cross-chain fee halves, from 0.2% to 0.1%. There is nothing to claim, stake or lock — the balance is read from the ledger when you connect, and the lower rate applies from then on.

Pool-held LUMOS counts

LUMOS sitting inside a liquidity pool counts toward the 250,000 threshold. Providing liquidity does not cost you the discount, which would otherwise punish the people doing the most useful thing with the token.

If your balance falls below the threshold the rate returns to 0.2%, and rises back to 0.1% the moment it does not. Nothing needs to be re-enabled.

Fees that are not ours

Two other costs appear on a transaction, and neither is charged by LumosCore:

  • The Stellar network fee. A fraction of a cent per operation, paid to the network, not to us. It applies to every transaction including the free ones.
  • Liquidity pool fees. Stellar's own pools charge 0.3% on a swap that routes through them, which is paid to the liquidity providers of that pool — that is the yield behind providing liquidity. Trades that route through the order book instead do not incur it.

On a cross-chain transfer you also pay the destination network's own fee, in that network's gas token, when the funds are claimed. On Ethereum mainnet that can exceed everything else on this page — see Cross-chain.

Where fees go

Platform fees settle on-chain, as a separate payment operation within the same transaction you signed. Because they are on-chain, they are auditable: the fee-collecting account and every payment into it can be inspected on any Stellar explorer, by anyone, without asking us.
